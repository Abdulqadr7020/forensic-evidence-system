# Roadmap (Milestone v1.0)

## Phase 1: Base Theming & Glassmorphism
**Goal:** Establish the core visual language (dark mode, glass effects, typography).
**Requirements:** 
- UI-01: Dark mode & glassmorphism theme

**Success Criteria:**
1. App renders entirely in dark mode by default.
2. Background uses a subtle dark surface or gradient appropriate for glassmorphism.
3. Primary layout panels and cards utilize a glass effect (backdrop blur + translucent borders and backgrounds).

## Phase 2: Component Standardization & Accents
**Goal:** Standardize the fundamental building blocks and inject vibrant accents.
**Requirements:** 
- UI-02: Vibrant accent colors
- UI-04: Standardize UI components

**Success Criteria:**
1. Buttons, inputs, and modals are unified and styled consistently across all views.
2. Primary actions and focus states use the chosen vibrant accent colors.
3. Forms in the Audit and Cases views share identical structure, spacing, and styling.

**Plans:** 2 plans
- [ ] 02-01-PLAN.md — Define Cyberpunk Tokens & Core Inputs (Input, Select, Textarea)
- [ ] 02-02-PLAN.md — Standardize Modals and Additional Interactive Elements

## Phase 3: Micro-interactions & Motion
**Goal:** Bring the UI to life with animations and fluid transitions.
**Requirements:** 
- UI-03: Micro-interactions via `motion`

**Success Criteria:**
1. Page/view transitions are smooth and animated.
2. Hover states on buttons, table rows, and interactive cards provide immediate, smooth visual feedback.
3. Click and focus events trigger subtle scale or color transition animations.

## Phase 4: Accessibility & Mobile Optimization
**Goal:** Ensure the polished UI is usable by everyone, everywhere.
**Requirements:** 
- UI-05: Accessibility and mobile responsiveness

**Success Criteria:**
1. Tab navigation works intuitively, clearly highlighting all interactive elements.
2. Contrast ratios across text and glassmorphic elements meet at least WCAG AA standards.
3. Layout adapts cleanly to mobile and tablet viewports without breaking the aesthetic or usability.
