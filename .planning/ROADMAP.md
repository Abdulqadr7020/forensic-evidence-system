# Roadmap (Milestone v1.1)

## Phase 5: Simplified Data Names
**Goal:** Rename all evidence files, case titles, and related terminology to be short, simple, and easy to understand in plain English.
**Requirements:** 
- DATA-01: Rename evidence files
- DATA-02: Rename case titles
- DATA-03: Remove complex jargon

**Success Criteria:**
1. All evidence items in the Integrity Verification and Documents views have simple names.
2. All case titles in the Cases view are short and understandable.
3. No cryptographic or developer jargon appears in the main user-facing mock data.
